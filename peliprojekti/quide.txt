Welcome!

In this game, your goal is to collect five pieces of trash from different locations around a city that you travel.
In the end, you will be graded and given an ending by how much trash you have collected.

You are asked different commmands to play the game:

- inventory, used to check what you have in your inventory
- surrounding, used to check what you can do in the current area
- statistics, used to check your own statistics
- move, used to move forward to new areas in the game
- cancel, used to exit the game and to either save or not your current progress

All you need is a working keyboard and computer to play the game. Enjoy!
